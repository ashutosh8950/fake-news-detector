# Task 56 — Draft PolitiFact-Oslo Rights Inquiry Package

**Status: DRAFT FOR OWNER REVIEW — NOT SENT**
**Prepared:** 2026-10-27
**Recipient/contact:** intentionally not populated; the owner must select the appropriate official contact after review.

## Purpose

TruthScan is an academic fake-news detection project with an existing article-level Fake/Real application interface. The proposed use of the PolitiFact-Oslo Corpus would be limited initially to an independently documented research and model-development assessment. The longer-term intended use is a public website accepting visitor-submitted articles and returning a non-definitive model prediction, subject to separate approval, rights review, trusted-artifact review, privacy controls, and release authorization.

No acquisition, scraping, retention, training, inference, model release, or deployment is authorized by this draft.

## Corpus identity currently verified

The reviewed institutional record and paper identify the **PolitiFact-Oslo Corpus: A New Dataset for Fake News Analysis and Detection**, by Nele Põldvere, Md Zia Uddin, and Aleena Thomas, affiliated with the University of Oslo and SINTEF Digital. The paper is published in *Information* 14(12), article 627, DOI `10.3390/info14120627`.

The paper describes a preliminary corpus of 2,745 texts covering January 2019–December 2022, with complete text items and metadata. It documents a broad grouping in which False/Mostly False/Pants on Fire become the broad False group and True/Mostly True/Half True become the broad True group; Full Flop and No Flip were excluded. The paper also describes manual selection of linked source material and occasional use of the Wayback Machine for corrected items.

This information identifies the published research corpus, but does not establish an immutable downloadable release, a local-file match, a dataset license, or authority to sublicense all retained material.

## Draft inquiry text

Subject: Request for clarification of rights and permitted uses for the PolitiFact-Oslo Corpus

Dear rights or data-use contact,

I am preparing an owner-reviewed, unsent inquiry on behalf of a project called TruthScan. TruthScan is an academic prototype for article-level Fake/Real text classification. We are evaluating whether the PolitiFact-Oslo Corpus could be used lawfully and reproducibly for a separately documented research track.

We are not requesting or using corpus material through this draft. Before any acquisition or implementation, could you please clarify the following for the exact PolitiFact-Oslo corpus described in the 2023 publication (DOI `10.3390/info14120627`)?

1. What exact corpus release, files, fields, and version identifier are covered by your response?
2. Does any permission cover only metadata/annotations, or does it also cover the complete article or social-media text described in the paper?
3. May the project obtain and retain the complete text for academic experimentation and internal evaluation?
4. May the project transform, normalize, tokenize, or otherwise preprocess that text for model development?
5. May the project train or fine-tune a model using the retained material?
6. May a model trained on that material be used in a public, non-commercial website that accepts visitor-submitted articles and returns a clearly labelled, non-definitive Fake/Real prediction?
7. If commercial use is contemplated later, is that permitted, and under what additional terms?
8. May trained model weights, checkpoints, evaluation artifacts, or derived metadata be distributed to others?
9. May raw or transformed corpus material be redistributed, included in a public repository, or stored in a public hosting environment?
10. What attribution, copyright notices, source links, access controls, retention periods, deletion procedures, refresh requirements, or other obligations apply?
11. Does any permission cover the linked third-party publisher material, images, social-media text, and copies obtained from an archival service, or must separate permissions be obtained from each relevant rights holder?
12. If separate permissions are required, which categories of rights holder must be consulted, and what records would demonstrate that the project’s intended uses are covered?

Please distinguish permissions for the dataset package, annotations, source metadata, complete article text, archived copies, and any social-platform material. We understand that a research paper, a DOI, or public web availability does not by itself establish permission for these uses.

TruthScan will not acquire, scrape, retain, train, publish, host, or deploy using this corpus until the applicable permissions and restrictions have been documented and reviewed by the project owner and a qualified rights reviewer. This inquiry is a request for clarification only and is not a commitment to use the material.

Thank you.

Sincerely,

[Owner to complete before any message is sent]
