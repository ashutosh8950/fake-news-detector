## Minimum evidence before acquisition

- Owner decision naming the candidate and permitted purpose.
- Version-specific rights review of the CC BY 4.0 assertion and legal code.
- Authenticated provenance statement identifying article sources, collection, transformations, and third-party exclusions.
- Written clarification or qualified review for public hosting, commercial use, model distribution, and raw-data redistribution.

## Minimum evidence before a college build

All acquisition evidence above, plus verified schema/labels, approved grouping and partitions, reproducible environment, secure preflight success, attribution/limitations text, and explicit build authorization.

## Minimum evidence before public hosting

All college-build evidence, plus explicit public-hosting and intended-commercial-use rights review, privacy/takedown/retention controls, artifact-integrity and security review, monitoring, release approval, and deployment authorization.
