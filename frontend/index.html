⚙️ How It Works

1
Advanced Preprocessing
Your text is cleaned, lemmatized with NLTK, and title+body are combined with 2× headline weighting.
2
Bigram TF-IDF Vectorization
Text is converted to a 100K-feature vector using bigram TF-IDF — capturing word pairs for richer patterns.
3
5-Model Ensemble
Five independent ML models vote on the article. The ensemble uses accuracy-weighted voting for the final call.
4
Confidence Score
A weighted confidence percentage is calculated from all model probabilities, so you know exactly how certain the AI is.
